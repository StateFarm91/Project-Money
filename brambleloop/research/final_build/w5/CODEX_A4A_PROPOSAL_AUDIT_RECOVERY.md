# A4a proposal audit recovery — partial review checkpoint

Base: `codex/a4a-census-price-validation` at `abba198416624930a38d54e10e10681f51e10279`.

A research worker could attach a proposal and stop before its gap update or `ideation.proposed` audit. Restart previously repaired the gap but never repaired that missing audit. The recovery path now checks a current research-job lease, gap/task ownership, the attached proposal and its persisted source/finding/census references. It updates the gap and writes the proposal audit in one database transaction; another cooperating recovery on the same gap sees the recorded event. Conflicting existing audit records cause a hold. The audit explicitly says it was recovered from persisted proposal data. Retention protects the event used for deduplication.

Python 3.11 file-backed SQLite: the A4a and retention-guard suites passed 43/43. PostgreSQL 16 on a disposable loopback server: the opt-in `test_codex_a4a_recovery_postgres.py` suite passed 6/6, including two separately claimed jobs calling the real registered handler concurrently. These tests include reassigned gap, missing audit, conflicting audit, inconsistent source reference and reclaimed worker lease. The real Worker restart tests use persistent state; the forced concurrent test invokes handlers with separately claimed jobs because Worker admission normally serializes the research agent.

Limits: the census digest and source/finding are consistent persisted records, not independently authenticated or a reconstruction of historical source rows. Mixed old and new workers, other writers that do not share these locks, possible lease expiry after the check, and task/gap reassignment after commit remain unfenced. Enqueueing intake happens after the transaction; intake itself does not yet revalidate ownership at its consequence. Stalled task reopening and A4b production enrichment remain unresolved. This is not full A4a integration or production certification. No production database or paid provider was used.
