- **Baskets as ONE listing for three sizes**: **OPEN.** This is not a K1 row. It is release-chain surgery across `listing.seo`, `assets.build`, `store.publish` (pipeline.py, a shared file) and delivery. Constraint found: Etsy allows **at most 5 files** per digital listing (`policy_knowledge` `digital_downloads`), and 3 sizes × US/UK PDFs is 6 files. The single listing therefore needs either 3 PDFs that each contain both US and UK terms, or 2 PDFs (US and UK) that each contain all three sizes. Proposed shape: a `product.assemble` handler modelled on `collection.assemble`. It would draft `Listing(product_slug="nursery-nesting-baskets", version="product")` from `seo.strategy.plan()["products"][0]` once all three variants are certified, and per-size drafts would be marked not-for-publish. The runtime proof below still shows per-size drafts, and the small basket's title reads "Hexagonal Bread Basket".
