**Acceptance.** "Use focused agents/workers for distinct domains such as Research,
Pattern/CIR Engineering, Visuals, QA, Etsy/Commerce, SEO/Marketing, Catalogue, Analytics,
Customer Experience, Finance/Cost, Reliability and Integration."

**Gap.** FMBATCH1 871b84a3 mapped the domains in `autonomy/departments.DOMAINS`. Analytics and
integration had no agent.

**Built.** No new department was added; there are still eleven charters, and
`charters.validate()` is clean. Two focused agents now sit in existing charters:

| domain | agent | charter(s) | work it now owns |
|---|---|---|---|
| analytics | `analyst` | Learn / Improvement (measurement already lived here) | the `capability_measure` cadence (`improve.measure`) |
| integration | `integration_steward` | Platform / Reliability; Store / Commerce | the `dependency_sweep` cadence (`ops.dependencies`) and the `etsy_openapi_reverify` cadence; also permitted `ops.provider_credentials` and `etsy.credential_health` |

  `cir.draft`, `cir.revise`, `cir.reengineer`, `gate.certify`, `store.publish`, `store.update`,
  `store.activate`, `ads.campaign` and `ads.adjust`.
- **Declared I/O:** both have `DECLARED_IO` entries.
- **No lost paths:** the orchestrator keeps its permission for these job types, so nothing
  that ran before loses a path.
