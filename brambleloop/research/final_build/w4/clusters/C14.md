| ID | built | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| F-766 | `support/project_tips.py`. Support cases (`failures_from_cases`) and tester failures are classified into the eight failure modes by fixed patterns, then grouped by product, component, row and mode. A tip is PROPOSED when there is one tester failure or 2+ distinct customers. It is placed at the named row and checked against the compiler: a stitch-count tip quotes the compiler's count, and a row the pattern does not have is REFUSED. A group with no row is NEEDS_PLACEMENT. No customer text is copied. | `test_f766_*` |
| F-769 | `support/gallery_evidence.py`. Product Truth is the verdict, and a gallery never changes it. A gallery never marks the physical test satisfied. It adds at most `GALLERY_WEIGHT_CAP` (0.2) of confidence, and only when it CORROBORATES: 2+ plausible images of this version from 2+ independent sources. An implausible image CONTRADICTS and opens a review. Images of other versions are set aside. | `test_f769_*` |
| F-802 | `support/lesson_trigger.py`. Questions, tickets, refunds, failed validations and review complaints are clustered by mode and product. Repeated confusion means 3+ distinct reporters, and it proposes a pre-registered experiment (hypothesis, metric, baseline, stop rule). The experiment is a pattern clarification when 2+ reporters share a row, a revision of an approved `Lesson` whose `spec.topics` covers `failure:<mode>` (at its revision hash), or otherwise a new lesson. It also queues the `LearnGap`, setting its state the way `learn.service.scan` does. The baseline is UNMEASURED when there is no order count. | `test_f802_*` |
| F-820 | `support/community.py`. Private signals (photos, support) are used only with learning consent; anything else counts as declined. No text is kept. Reporters are salted digests. Photo refs are kept only with reuse consent. Aggregates with 3+ contributors are published to the lesson bus (`improve.bus`, origin `customer_experience`, routed to Learn) and are idempotent on the aggregate key. | `test_f820_*` |
| F-825 | `analytics/content_economics.py`. Cost is creation plus maintenance times months live. Value is attributable sales plus the proxies at stated unit values with a basis; an unvalued or unmeasured part is UNKNOWN, never 0. The verdict (KEEP, REDESIGN, RETIRE or UNKNOWN) is given per item and per format after 3 months live; RETIRE applies below 25% of cost, or when an item is still below cost after a redesign. These are recommendations only. | `test_f825_*` |

## Dependency note

The cluster lists C11's Lesson.spec as a dependency for F-766 and F-802. It already exists on
INTEG7 (`learn.models.Lesson.spec`, with the revision equal to the sha256 of the spec), so C14
did not wait for C11. F-802 reads `spec.topics` from approved lessons through
`learn.service.eligible`.
