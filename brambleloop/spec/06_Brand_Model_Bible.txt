BRAMBLELOOP BRAND MODEL — INITIAL CHARACTER BIBLE
=================================================
Purpose:
A consistent fictional adult model for wearables/accessories so the Etsy grid looks like one premium design house.

Direction:
- adult woman
- glamorous brunette
- long rich dark-brown hair
- striking light eyes
- strong defined brows
- refined, balanced facial features
- polished editorial beauty
- warm, approachable expression
- tasteful natural-to-polished makeup
- premium but relatable lifestyle presentation
- clothing appropriate to the crochet garment, season and setting

Continuity requirements:
- preserve the same fictional identity across generations
- maintain stable face proportions, eye colour, hair colour/length, body proportions and approximate age
- store approved reference assets and generation metadata
- reject material character drift
- do not duplicate Megan Fox or any other real person; the desired direction is a general high-glamour brunette aesthetic
- do not use the model where product-first imagery is stronger (blankets, table decor, amigurumi, etc.)

Visual brand:
- warm cream / forest / wine / muted gold / botanical accents
- premium editorial, cozy, heirloom, clean
- avoid generic AI-store appearance

- "An image depicting a woman similar to Laura is NOT Laura and fails the identity gate."
