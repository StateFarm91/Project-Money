# DECISION_LOG — Brambleloop Studio

Consequential decisions with reasoning, so future sessions do not re-litigate them.

| # | Date | Decision | Why | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-001 | 2026-09-17 | Project-Money 90-day competition retired; Brambleloop Studio is the active mission. | Owner directive: "Scrap the competition. Competition is cancelled." Handoff package v1.2 supplied as canonical spec. | Owner's call |
| B-002 | 2026-09-17 | Python 3.11 for the core, not Node/TS. | The differentiating work is deterministic arithmetic, PDF/chart generation and image compositing. Python's reportlab/Pillow are already proven working in this environment. A JS frontend is not needed for an admin dashboard. | Yes, but costly |
| B-003 | 2026-09-17 | Postgres-backed durable queue (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`), not Redis/Celery/SQS. | Master Plan section 13 demands durable jobs with leases, retries and idempotency. Postgres already has to exist for the warehouse; adding a second datastore adds a component that can die unattended at 3am with nobody watching. Fewer moving parts is a reliability decision, not a laziness one. | Yes |
| B-004 | 2026-09-17 | SQLite for tests, Postgres for production, behind one abstraction. | Acceptance tests must run hermetically anywhere, including before any cloud account exists. SKIP LOCKED degrades to a transactional claim on SQLite. | Yes |
| B-005 | 2026-09-17 | Writer and reverse compiler share **no** parsing code. | If they shared a grammar, a round-trip test would prove only that the bug is symmetric. Master Plan section 3 requires the reverse compiler to see "only the customer pattern". Independence is the whole point. | No — this is load-bearing |
| B-006 | 2026-09-17 | Digital twin refuses to model a pattern that failed compilation. | A twin built on broken arithmetic is fiction, and every downstream gate (Asset Truth, size claims, yardage) would then be reasoning about an object that cannot exist. | No |
| B-007 | 2026-09-17 | Yardage is an estimate with an explicit ±20% tolerance and a `calibrated` flag. | Yarn consumption genuinely varies by yarn, hook and tension. Inventing a precise number would be the exact "unsupported claim" the Policy Gate exists to block. Physical tests calibrate it (Master Plan section 3). | Yes, as data arrives |
| B-008 | 2026-09-17 | Magic ring modelled as a `foundation_kind`, not a free-text note. | The writer was emitting "sc in next 6 sts" for a magic-ring round — nonsense to a maker. An override string would have created a hole in reverse-compilation verification; a structural field keeps the check intact. | Yes |
| B-009 | 2026-09-17 | Built under `brambleloop/` in the Project-Money repo rather than a new repo. | Push credentials are scoped to `StateFarm91/Project-Money`. Blocking real progress on repo cosmetics would violate the directive's "do not stop because one integration is unavailable; route around it". Migration to a dedicated repo is trivial later and is tracked in BUILD_STATE. | Yes |
| B-010 | 2026-09-17 | Cloud not provisioned yet despite a verified Railway account. | Master Plan section 35 defers paid infrastructure approval until the system reaches the integration that needs it. Provisioning an empty project now would spend the owner's money to host nothing. Deploy when there is a service worth running. | Yes |
| B-011 | 2026-09-17 | Opportunity scoring is a weighted sum of six named components, computed in code. No model ranks concepts. | A model is good at proposing concepts and unreliable at being consistent about why one beats another. "The model preferred it" is not a reason the owner can overrule six weeks later when a SKU underperforms. Every score decomposes into demand, competition headroom, verifiability, seasonal fit, margin and whitespace, and the weights are one editable dict. | Yes — weights are data |
| B-012 | 2026-09-17 | Seasonal fit is evaluated at **our ready date**, using **the concept's own** maker hours, not the event date and not generic ones. | Two separate errors this avoids. Ranking by days-until-the-holiday would schedule Christmas blankets for December, when no buyer can finish one. Using the event's generic make time would give a stocking and a throw the same deadline, and they are six weeks apart. Scoring at today's date rather than the date we could actually list is a third: a concept we cannot ship for three weeks must be judged on the market that will exist in three weeks. | Yes |
| B-013 | 2026-09-17 | Portfolio selection applies structural constraints and will deliberately take a lower-scoring concept to meet them. | Section 33 forbids a first portfolio that depends on a single demand pattern. Pure ranking produced four Christmas items and no fast movers. Every swap is recorded with both scores and the constraint it served, so the trade is visible rather than buried. | Yes |
| B-014 | 2026-09-17 | Bundles are derived inventory: excluded from the ranked competition, appended only when their family earned at least two places. | The bundle scores highest in the pool because it is cheap and high-margin — but only once its members exist. Ranking it as an independent SKU produced a portfolio containing a bundle of products we had not built, which is a listing we cannot fulfil. | Yes |
| B-015 | 2026-09-17 | Competitor profiles carry an observation date and a URL, and are asserted on in tests. | Market evidence rots silently. An undated price band read as current a year later is worse than no data, because it looks like data. | No |
| B-016 | 2026-09-17 | Stitch taxonomy carries `height` (turning-chain units) and `row_height` (physical) as separate fields. | They were one field. A dc turns with three chains and is about twice the height of a sc; conflating them overstated a blanket's finished length by half. Finished length is a size claim on a listing, so this was a refund waiting to be printed, not a rounding issue. | No |
| B-017 | 2026-09-17 | Yarn constants are anchored on a checkable reference, and the anchor is written down next to them. | The first table was set by feel and came out at less than half of a defensible figure — a throw would have been advertised as needing 190 m. An estimate is allowed to be wrong by its stated tolerance; being wrong by a factor of two is not an estimate. Still uncalibrated at +/-20% until physical tests replace it. | Yes, on evidence |
| B-018 | 2026-09-17 | The flagship's pattern is generated from a motif grid, and each row is emitted as a repeat rather than flattened. | Flattening twelve repeats of a twelve-stitch motif produced a row instruction eleven lines long: physically unusable, and the kind of thing that makes a maker lose their place and blame the pattern. The repeat form is both what a human follows and what the compiler checks for divisibility, so readable and verifiable are the same form. | Yes |
| B-019 | 2026-09-17 | Large charts are published as one detected repeat plus a placement note, never as the whole piece. | A 144 x 120 chart on one page gives each stitch about a pixel. The repeat is *computed* from the fabric rather than asserted in copy, so the chart cannot claim a repeat the pattern does not have. | Yes |
| B-020 | 2026-09-17 | Web, worker and scheduler run in one container for now. | Railway bills by resources consumed and the company has no revenue; three always-on services cost roughly three times one. Correctness does not depend on the split — the queue is durable with leases and idempotency keys — so splitting later is a start command, and `worker_entry.py` and `scheduler_entry.py` already exist for that day. | Yes |
| B-021 | 2026-09-17 | With `BRAMBLELOOP_REQUIRE_POSTGRES=1` the app refuses to start rather than fall back to SQLite. | A container that silently falls back to a file on ephemeral disk passes every health check, renders the dashboard, and destroys every job, audit record and release certificate on its next restart. Failing to start is enormously preferable to a company whose memory quietly resets. | No |
| B-022 | 2026-09-17 | Artifact bytes are stored on local disk; artifact hashes are stored in Postgres. | No object storage is provisioned. The split means the system can always prove the PDF it is about to ship is the one that was certified, and when a file is gone it knows it is gone instead of serving a different one. Every run audits `assets.storage_not_durable` rather than implying durability it does not have. | Yes, swap in S3 |
| B-023 | 2026-09-17 | Prompts are immutable, content-hashed and referenced as `name@version`. | A prompt is a dependency, not a string. If it can be edited in place then two runs of the same job are not comparable, a regression cannot be attributed, and the audit trail records a call to something that no longer exists. Editing means publishing a new version; the old text stays so last month's output can still be explained. | No |
| B-024 | 2026-09-17 | No registered prompt may ask a model for pattern content, and this is enforced by a test. | Section 2 is absolute and a standing constraint is worth nothing if it lives only in a docstring. The prompt set is deliberately narrow: naming, readability polish that may not change a fact, and review mining. Everything load-bearing stays deterministic. | No |
| B-025 | 2026-09-17 | `ModelGateway.require_deterministic()` raises rather than returning a model verdict. | Section 27 says deterministic validation wins even if every LLM disagrees. Making it a method rather than a comment means a future caller reaching for a model opinion on pattern correctness gets an exception instead of a code review that may not happen. | No |
| B-026 | 2026-09-17 | The gateway ships with a deterministic `EchoProvider` and reports zero real providers. | No API key exists in this environment. Echo mocks the transport — pinning, parsing, cost, failover, circuit breaking — never judgement, which eval fixtures test instead. `available_providers()` returning an empty list is the truthful answer, and the dashboard shows it. | Yes, when a key exists |
| B-027 | 2026-09-17 | Agent permissions and ceilings are declarative: `seed_defaults` reconciles them on every boot. | Insert-if-absent meant a permission fixed in code never reached a database that already had the agent. Production proved it: the heartbeat fix deployed and the heartbeat kept failing. A permission that lives in code and cannot reach the database is not a permission, it is a comment. Runtime state such as `enabled` is left alone. | No |
| B-028 | 2026-09-17 | The container becomes healthy before it takes on load (`BRAMBLELOOP_RUNNER_START_DELAY`). | Two deployments were marked failed by the platform while working perfectly: the embedded worker began a full planning cycle at startup, and rendering eleven products' PDFs is CPU-bound work holding the GIL, which starved the health endpoint past the timeout. Serving first and working second is how a container is supposed to start. | Yes |
| B-029 | 2026-09-17 | Dead letters can be re-driven, but a closed capability gate can never be. | A dead letter from a fixed defect is work the company still owes, and leaving it red for a day teaches an operator to ignore the signal. Re-driving a shadow-mode publish refusal, by contrast, is an operator repeatedly asking a closed gate to open. A permission denial IS re-drivable: it reads like a refusal and is usually a misconfiguration, and the worker re-authorises on dispatch so a still-missing permission simply fails again. | Yes |
| B-030 | 2026-09-17 | `/api/verify` evaluates the standing safety assertions against live state and returns 503 if any fails. | "Railway says deployed" is not "the company is alive and behaving". The questions an absent owner actually has — is it running, is its memory durable, has anything escaped shadow mode, are the money guards on — are now answerable in one request, with the evidence for each check attached so a green result can be argued with. | No |
| B-031 | 2026-09-17 | The brand is data — palette, type, crop rules, naming grammar, character bible — not taste. | Consistency maintained by judgement alone does not survive a system producing assets unattended for months. `check_grid_coherence` can say whether the storefront still reads as one shop without anyone looking at it. | Yes |
| B-032 | 2026-09-17 | The brand model is refused for product-first categories, refused any real-person likeness, and can never be the hero. | Section 6 says product-first imagery is stronger for blankets and decor, and a model holding a blanket hides the pattern being sold. A likeness of a real person is refused however the request is phrased. An AI lifestyle concept as hero would be a picture of a thing nobody has made presented as evidence the thing exists. | No |
| B-033 | 2026-09-17 | Listing imagery is part of the release: rendered from the twin, class-declared, Asset Truth-checked, and blocking. | Imagery assembled after certification is imagery nothing verified. Rendering every frame from the same twin as the PDF means no frame can assert something the pattern does not produce, and a listing whose imagery misrepresents the pattern now stops the chain before pricing. | No |
| B-034 | 2026-09-17 | Search coverage is scored against *reachable* queries, not the biggest ones. | A shop with no history is not placing for "crochet blanket pattern", and scoring against it would tell the system to spend all thirteen tag slots on queries it will never appear for. Technique tags are gated on techniques the pattern actually uses: ranking for something we are not is a bounce, not a win. | Yes |
| B-035 | 2026-09-17 | Pricing optimises expected contribution per visitor, never gross revenue or lowest price. | A cheaper pattern converts better and can still earn less per visitor; a dearer one can earn more in total while killing the review accumulation a new shop depends on. Every figure is net of Etsy's real fee stack, because a decision made on gross is made on a number that never reaches the bank. | Yes |
| B-036 | 2026-09-17 | A price experiment's stopping rule is fixed before it starts, and a sub-8% price difference is refused at design time. | A test whose stopping rule is decided while looking at the results is a way of confirming what the operator already believed. At the sample sizes a new shop can reach, a 3% price difference is unmeasurable. | No |
| B-037 | 2026-09-17 | Thumbnail composition is checked now; hero ranking waits for real impressions and refuses without them. | Section 7 asks for both. The compositional half works today because a hero can be downscaled to Etsy's real grid size and measured. The evidence half cannot: inventing plausible CTRs to make a ranking function return something is the fabricated engagement section 8 forbids, applied to ourselves. | Yes, when data exists |
| B-038 | 2026-09-17 | Support triage puts escalation first in the routing table, and every reply is recorded as unsent. | A message containing both "chargeback" and "yarn" is a dispute; ordering is what stops a legal matter reaching the materials desk. And "we could not have sent it" is a weaker guarantee than "we recorded that we did not". | No |
| B-039 | 2026-09-17 | The portfolio classifier returns NO_EVIDENCE rather than a diagnosis for a SKU nobody has seen. | Every SKU has zero impressions. A classifier that read that as CONVERSION_PROBLEM would retire a catalogue that has simply never been shown to anyone — the most expensive mistake available to this component. | No |
| B-040 | 2026-09-17 | Confidence is tracked as seven separate dimensions with deliberately no overall score. | An average is more confident than the guess and less confident than the proof. Arithmetic confidence is near-total and yardage confidence is 0.45; averaging them would let a listing imply the yarn figure is as solid as the stitch counts. Physical confidence starts at zero and no amount of computation moves it. | No |
| B-041 | 2026-09-17 | Regression fixtures store the defective pattern, not the fix, and a clean pattern cannot become one. | A suite made of fixed patterns proves only that the fixed patterns are still fixed. A fixture that provokes no finding asserts nothing, and a suite full of those reports green while testing nothing. | No |
| B-042 | 2026-09-17 | Products are generated from a validated motif library; a width the motif cannot tile is refused. | Engineering twelve products by hand is twelve chances to slip. A truncated motif compiles perfectly and leaves half a fir tree down one side — a defect no arithmetic will ever catch, so the builder refuses rather than fudges. | Yes |
| B-043 | 2026-09-17 | A bundle has no pattern of its own and is assembled from its members' certified releases. | The pipeline had been certifying a "Nordic Forest Collection Bundle" whose PDF was a twelve-row striped panel: a product sold as three patterns and delivered as one invented swatch. A collection now lists only members that actually shipped, and waits when fewer than two have. | No |
| B-044 | 2026-09-17 | The books are built while every figure is zero, and the tax reserve is never counted as ours. | Accounting that first appears alongside the first sale is accounting nobody has checked; accounting built while the answer is obviously zero can be checked against an answer everyone knows. GST/HST is money held for a government, and counting it as revenue is how a small business discovers a liability it has already spent. | No |
| B-045 | 2026-09-17 | The CA$100K trajectory refuses to produce a run rate or a projection from zero orders. | It is the number most likely to be quoted back as though it were a forecast. With no orders there is no run rate; what the function returns instead is arithmetic on the target, labelled as arithmetic. | No |
| B-046 | 2026-09-17 | The CFO/Skeptic reports concerns even when nothing is wrong. | A reviewer who only speaks up during a crisis is a reviewer nobody has calibrated. It blocks on infrastructure above the owner's ceiling and on advertising with no attributable orders, and notes the burn and the tax reserve every time. | Yes |
| B-047 | 2026-09-17 | Paid-media guard rails are built now, while no campaign exists and no money can be spent. | Advertising is the one part of this system that can lose money quickly and silently. Guard rails written after the first campaign are guard rails written by someone who already knows what they cost. `authorise_spend` refuses on phase and on missing owner authority before it looks at any budget: a cap is not permission. | No |
| B-048 | 2026-09-17 | Broken tracking pauses a campaign before any budget question is asked. | A campaign running with broken attribution is not one that might be losing money; it is one nobody can tell is losing money, and a budget cap does not protect against being blind. | No |
| B-049 | 2026-09-17 | Every acceptance-gate line has its own named test. | "Gate B passes" was previously inferred from a scattering of component tests. Now each line of the owner's acceptance document has a test that names it, so a gate claim is checkable, and a second Gate F test asserts that nothing actually graduated. | No |
| B-050 | 2026-09-17 | Difficulty claims are checked in both directions. | The rule only caught "beginner" on a Class C pattern, which left the gate line "unsupported difficulty claims are blocked" half true. Overstating difficulty is just as unsupported as understating it, and it turns away the buyer the product was designed for. | Yes |
| B-051 | 2026-09-17 | Every idempotency key after certification carries a chain version. | Production proved the need: fifteen products were certified before listing imagery, search coverage and the content ecosystem existed, and the next cycle collapsed every downstream job against keys that were already taken — reporting success and producing nothing. Certification is correctly once per version; everything after it is different work when the code that does it changes. | Yes |
| B-052 | 2026-09-17 | `chain.rebuild` runs hourly and restarts certified releases that have no current listing. | Bumping the chain version makes the keys free; something still has to notice. A certified product with no listing is a product that cannot be sold, which is worth checking for on a cadence rather than only after a deploy. | Yes |

## Standing constraints carried from the spec

These are not re-decidable by a future session without the owner:

- Patterns are software releases. Never generate a beauty image and ask a model to guess the
  instructions (section 2).
- No fake reviews, buyers, favourites, sock-puppets, deceptive discounts or artificial
  engagement (Execution Directive, section 8).
- Never bypass CAPTCHA, KYC, identity checks or legal acceptance.
- Never copy competitor instructions, charts, photography or protected designs. Research is
  for demand and merchandising intelligence only.
- Never claim an integration, deployment, pattern, listing, test, campaign, customer or
  revenue exists until verified.
- Deterministic pattern validation wins even if every LLM disagrees (section 27).
