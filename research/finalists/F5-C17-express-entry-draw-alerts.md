## Why screened out
Every credible first-10 path (immigration subreddits, Discords, Telegram groups) requires community posting from the owner's accounts, which the operator cannot do and the owner should not have to. SEO for draw results is owned by consultancies. The product is fully automatable and cheap (CA$60), but distribution is not solved with the product.

## Numbers (CAD)
P(first revenue ≤30d) ≈ 30% **[high]**. Day-90: downside 0 · base 250 · upside 1,500 · net ≈ base 200. Failure ≈ 60%. Day-180 **[med]** (audience asset if a Telegram/Discord bot spreads by word of mouth).

## Decision
Backlog. Revisit only if a distribution mechanism the operator can run appears (e.g. a Telegram bot listed in bot directories with organic pickup).
