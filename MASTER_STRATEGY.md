## Economic thesis (provisional, pre-research)

- Capital is small ($1,000 CAD max) and owner time is the scarcest resource, so the winning shape is a product that Claude can build, operate, and market almost entirely through code, APIs, and scheduled automation, with a self-serve payment path.
- Distribution must be solved with the product: prefer channels that already carry buyer intent (marketplaces, app stores, search, existing communities) over channels that require the owner to sell.
- Gross revenue is only useful if it nets positive after fees; prefer high-gross-margin digital offers.

Everything above is an assumption until the research in `research/` confirms or refutes it.
