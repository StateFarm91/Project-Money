## About — story
MapleSheets makes bookkeeping and tax-prep spreadsheets for Canadian freelancers, contractors, Etsy and Shopify sellers and other sole proprietors. Every workbook maps to CRA's Form T2125, handles sales tax for every province and territory (GST, HST, PST, RST, QST), and works in both Excel and Google Sheets. We check the rates against CRA's published tables for each tax year, test every formula before release, and update editions as the rules change. We are a small Canadian shop and use AI tools to help build and check the formulas; the templates are designed, reviewed and supported by us. Our templates are bookkeeping tools, not tax advice — for anything that affects your return, please confirm with the CRA or an accountant.

## Shop policies
**Digital downloads.** All products are instant digital downloads (ZIP with an .xlsx workbook, a PDF guide and a README). No physical item is shipped. Files are available from your Etsy purchases page right after payment.

**Compatibility.** Workbooks open in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer and import into Google Sheets (File > Import > Replace spreadsheet). They do not use macros or add-ons. Numbers and Apple Pages are not supported.

**Returns and refunds.** Because the files are digital, Etsy does not process returns automatically. If a workbook does not work for you, message us within 14 days of purchase with a screenshot of the problem: we will fix it, send an updated file, or refund you. We do not argue about refunds.

**Updates.** Each edition is dated to a tax year. Buyers of an edition receive bug-fix updates for that edition through the Etsy purchases page. New tax-year editions are separate purchases.

**Support.** Message the shop through Etsy. We usually reply within one business day.

**Not advice.** Our products are bookkeeping tools and templates. They are not tax, legal or accounting advice and do not replace an accountant or the CRA's own guidance. Rates and line numbers are checked at release; verify anything that affects your return before filing.

**Licence.** Personal and small-business use by the purchaser. Please do not resell, share or republish the files.

## FAQ (optional, Etsy "Frequently asked questions")
- **Does it work in Google Sheets?** Yes. Import the .xlsx (File > Import > Upload > Replace spreadsheet). Dropdowns and formulas carry over.
- **Which provinces?** All 13 provinces and territories; pick yours on the Start here sheet.
- **I am not registered for GST/HST.** Set "Registered" to No; the tax columns stay at zero and the small-supplier tracker tells you when you must register.
- **Is this tax advice?** No. It is a bookkeeping tool; confirm anything that affects your return with the CRA or an accountant.
